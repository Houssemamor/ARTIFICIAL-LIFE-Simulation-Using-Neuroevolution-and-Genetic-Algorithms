Neuroevolution Showcase

Small neural-network agents evolved by a genetic algorithm. Watch the best brains act, look inside them, then see what each experiment found.

1. Watch the evolved agents

A recording of the real simulation, replayed here. Pick a world and compare untrained random brains with evolved ones on the same arena and start positions.

2. Look inside the brain

Each agent is a small network (12 sensors, 32 and 16 hidden units, 3 actions). Blue lines push a unit up, orange lines push it down; moving dots show the signals flowing from sensors to actions for the featured agent, step by step.

3. What the experiments found

Each card says what was tested and what was measured. Verdicts are computed from the statistics: a result only counts as supported when it passes the significance test, and many here are trends that need more data.
